# Question

An online retail application experiences significant and unpredictable spikes in traffic during holiday sales events. The application needs to automatically increase its computing resources to handle the increased load and then decrease resources when the traffic subsides, all without manual intervention. Which cloud benefit is most relevant to this requirement?

## Choices

A. High Availability
> High availability ensures continuous operation and minimizes downtime, but it does not specifically address dynamic resource scaling based on demand.
B. Reliability
> Reliability ensures consistent performance and uptime, but it does not inherently mean automatic scaling up and down with fluctuating demand.
C. Elasticity *[CORRECT]*
> Elasticity specifically refers to the ability to automatically scale resources up or down in response to changes in demand.
D. Disaster Recovery
> Disaster recovery focuses on restoring operations after a major outage or disaster, not on dynamic resource allocation for fluctuating workloads.
