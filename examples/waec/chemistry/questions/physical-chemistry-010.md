explanation: |
  Real gases deviate from ideal gas behavior under conditions of high pressure and low temperature. At high pressures, the volume occupied by gas molecules themselves becomes significant compared to the total volume, contradicting the ideal gas assumption of negligible molecular volume. At low temperatures, intermolecular forces become more significant, which are ignored in the ideal gas model.
