explanation: |
  A coordinate covalent bond (or dative bond) is a type of covalent bond where one atom provides both electrons for the shared pair. In the ammonium ion (NH₄⁺), the nitrogen atom in ammonia (NH₃) donates its lone pair of electrons to form a bond with a hydrogen ion (H⁺), which has no electrons to share.
