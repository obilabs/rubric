# Question
An atom of an element has 6 protons, 6 electrons, and 8 neutrons. Which of the following statements is TRUE about this atom?
## Choices
A. Its mass number is 14. *[CORRECT]*
B. Its atomic number is 8.
C. It is an isotope of an element with 8 protons.
D. It has a net positive charge.
