  When solid urea, a common fertilizer used by Nigerian farmers, dissolves in water, the surrounding solution feels cold to the touch. This indicates that the process absorbs heat from the surroundings. A process that absorbs heat from its surroundings is classified as an endothermic process.

B. Endothermic process. *[CORRECT]*
