  The rate of a chemical reaction is given by the rate law, Rate = k[A]^x[B]^y. If doubling the concentration of A (while keeping B constant) quadruples the reaction rate, then (2)^x = 4, which means x = 2. Therefore, the reaction is second order with respect to A.
