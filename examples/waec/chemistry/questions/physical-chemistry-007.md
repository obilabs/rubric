explanation: |
  According to Graham's Law of Diffusion, the rate of diffusion of a gas is inversely proportional to the square root of its molar mass.
  Given Rate(H₂) / Rate(X) = 4, and Molar Mass(H₂) = 2 g/mol.
  (Rate(H₂) / Rate(X)) = √(Molar Mass(X) / Molar Mass(H₂))
  4 = √(Molar Mass(X) / 2)
  Squaring both sides: 16 = Molar Mass(X) / 2
  Molar Mass(X) = 16 × 2 = 32 g/mol. This corresponds to oxygen gas (O₂).

# Question
Hydrogen gas diffuses four times faster than an unknown gas 'X' under the same conditions. What is the molar mass of gas 'X'? (H = 1 g/mol)
## Choices
A. 8 g/mol
B. 32 g/mol *[CORRECT]*
C. 16 g/mol
D. 64 g/mol
