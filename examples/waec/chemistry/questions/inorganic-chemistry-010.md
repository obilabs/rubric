  Groundnuts generally thrive in slightly acidic to neutral soils. If the soil is too acidic (pH 5.0), it needs to be made less acidic (i.e., its pH increased). Slaked lime (calcium hydroxide) is a basic substance commonly added to acidic soils to neutralize the acidity and raise the pH to a more suitable level for crop growth.
