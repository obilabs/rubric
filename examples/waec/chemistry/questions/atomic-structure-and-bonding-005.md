explanation: |
  A molecule is considered polar if it contains polar bonds and its molecular geometry is asymmetrical, leading to a net dipole moment. H₂O has polar O-H bonds and a bent shape, making it polar. CO₂ has polar C=O bonds but a linear shape, so the dipoles cancel out, making it non-polar. CH₄ and CCl₄ have symmetrical tetrahedral shapes, making them non-polar despite having polar C-H and C-Cl bonds, respectively.
