  According to VSEPR theory, the central oxygen atom in a water molecule (H₂O) has two bonding pairs and two lone pairs of electrons. These four electron domains arrange themselves in a tetrahedral electron geometry, but the presence of the two lone pairs repels the bonding pairs more strongly, distorting the shape and resulting in a bent or V-shape molecular geometry with an approximate bond angle of 104.5°.
