explanation: |
  The atomic number (Z) represents the number of protons and, in a neutral atom, the number of electrons. The mass number (A) is the sum of protons and neutrons. For Carbon-14, Z=6, so it has 6 protons and 6 electrons. The number of neutrons = A - Z = 14 - 6 = 8.

# Question
Carbon-14 (¹⁴₆C) is an isotope of carbon commonly used in dating ancient Nigerian artifacts. How many protons, neutrons, and electrons are present in a neutral atom of Carbon-14 respectively?
## Choices
A. 6 protons, 8 neutrons, 6 electrons *[CORRECT]*
B. 6 protons, 6 neutrons, 8 electrons
C. 8 protons, 6 neutrons, 8 electrons
D. 14 protons, 6 neutrons, 14 electrons
