  According to Charles's Law, for a fixed mass of gas at constant pressure, the volume is directly proportional to its absolute temperature. Therefore, increasing the temperature will cause the volume of the gas to increase.
---
# Question
A flexible balloon containing a fixed mass of gas is taken from a cold morning in Jos to a hot afternoon in Abuja, assuming the pressure remains constant. What will happen to the volume of the gas in the balloon?
## Choices
A. It will increase. *[CORRECT]*
B. It will decrease.
C. It will remain constant.
D. It will first decrease then increase.
