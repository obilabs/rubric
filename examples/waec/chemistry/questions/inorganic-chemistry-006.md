  A more reactive metal can displace a less reactive metal from its salt solution. The observation of a reddish-brown deposit (copper metal) on the zinc indicates that zinc has displaced copper from the copper(II) sulphate solution, thus zinc is more reactive than copper.

# Question
A piece of zinc metal is dipped into a solution of copper(II) sulphate. A reddish-brown deposit is observed on the zinc. This reaction demonstrates that:
## Choices
A. Copper is more reactive than zinc
B. Zinc is more reactive than copper *[CORRECT]*
C. Both metals have equal reactivity
D. The solution is neutral
