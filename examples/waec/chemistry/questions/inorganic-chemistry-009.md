  Aluminium is a highly reactive metal and therefore cannot be extracted by reduction with carbon. Its extraction from purified bauxite (alumina) is carried out by electrolysis of molten aluminium oxide, dissolved in cryolite, in a process known as the Hall-Heroult process.
