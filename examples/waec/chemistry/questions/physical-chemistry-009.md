  In an electrochemical (voltaic/galvanic) cell, the anode is where oxidation occurs (loss of electrons) and is the negative terminal. The cathode is where reduction occurs (gain of electrons) and is the positive terminal. Electrons always flow from the anode to the cathode through the external circuit.
