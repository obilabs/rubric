  Propan-2-ol is a secondary alkanol because the carbon atom bearing the hydroxyl (-OH) group is directly attached to two other carbon atoms. In ethanol and propan-1-ol, the -OH group is on a carbon attached to only one other carbon (primary), while 2-methylpropan-2-ol is tertiary.
