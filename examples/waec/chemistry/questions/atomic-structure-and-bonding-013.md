explanation: |
  Table salt (NaCl) is an ionic compound formed by the transfer of an electron from sodium (a metal) to chlorine (a non-metal). This creates positively charged sodium ions (Na⁺) and negatively charged chloride ions (Cl⁻), which are held together by strong electrostatic forces. Sugar, water, and cooking gas (butane) are covalent compounds, held together by shared electrons.
