explanation: |
  Halogens are elements in Group 17 of the periodic table (e.g., F, Cl, Br, I). They are highly reactive non-metals, readily gaining one electron to achieve a stable octet, forming -1 ions (halides). They exist as diatomic molecules (e.g., Cl₂, Br₂) in their elemental state. Alkali metals (Group 1) are highly reactive metals. Noble gases (Group 18) are largely unreactive. Transition metals (d-block) are characterized by variable oxidation states.
