  According to Le Chatelier's principle, for a gaseous equilibrium, increasing the pressure will shift the equilibrium towards the side with fewer moles of gas to relieve the stress. In this reaction, the reactants have 4 moles of gas (1 N₂ + 3 H₂) while the product has 2 moles of gas (2 NH₃). Therefore, increasing the pressure favors the formation of more ammonia.
