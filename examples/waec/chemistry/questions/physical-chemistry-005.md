  According to Le Chatelier's principle, to maximize the yield of ammonia (an exothermic reaction that produces fewer moles of gas), the equilibrium should shift to the right. This is achieved by increasing pressure (favors fewer moles of gas) and decreasing temperature (favors exothermic reaction).
