  First, calculate the total charge passed: Q = I × t = 5 A × 19300 s = 96500 C.
  The reduction half-reaction for copper is Cu²⁺ + 2e⁻ → Cu.
  This means 2 moles of electrons (2 × 96500 C = 193000 C) are required to deposit 1 mole of copper (63.5 g).
  Therefore, if 193000 C deposits 63.5 g, then 96500 C will deposit (96500 C / 193000 C) × 63.5 g = 0.5 × 63.5 g = 31.75 g.

# Question
