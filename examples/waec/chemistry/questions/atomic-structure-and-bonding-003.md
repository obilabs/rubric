  Electronegativity generally increases across a period from left to right and decreases down a group. Fluorine (F) is in Period 2, Group 17, and is the most electronegative element. Chlorine (Cl) is in Period 3, Group 17. Bromine (Br) is in Period 4, Group 17. Silicon (Si) is in Period 3, Group 14. Therefore, Fluorine has the highest electronegativity among the options.
