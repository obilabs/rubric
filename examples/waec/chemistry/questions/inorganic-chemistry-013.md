  Alum (aluminium sulphate) acts as a coagulant in water purification. It hydrolyses to form gelatinous aluminium hydroxide, which traps and binds tiny suspended colloidal particles together (flocculation), making them heavy enough to settle out.

C. coagulate suspended colloidal particles. *[CORRECT]*
