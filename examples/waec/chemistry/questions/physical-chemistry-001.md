explanation: |
  Boyle's Law states that for a fixed mass of gas at constant temperature, the pressure is inversely proportional to its volume (P₁V₁ = P₂V₂).
  Given P₁ = 1.5 atm, V₁ = 30 L, and V₂ = 10 L.
  So, P₂ = (P₁V₁) / V₂ = (1.5 atm * 30 L) / 10 L = 4.5 atm.
---
# Question
A gas in a cylinder at a petrol station in Kaduna has a volume of 30 L at a pressure of 1.5 atm. If the gas is compressed at constant temperature to a volume of 10 L, what will be its new pressure?
## Choices
A. 0.5 atm
B. 4.5 atm *[CORRECT]*
C. 2.0 atm
D. 3.0 atm
