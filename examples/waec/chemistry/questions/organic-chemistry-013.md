  Esterification is the reaction between an alkanoic acid and an alkanol in the presence of a strong acid catalyst (like concentrated H₂SO₄) to form an ester and water. Ethanoic acid (CH₃COOH) reacting with propan-1-ol (CH₃CH₂CH₂OH) forms propyl ethanoate (CH₃COOCH₂CH₂CH₃) and water.
