  The combination of greenish-white diarrhoea, neurological signs (twisted neck, paralysis), and a severe drop in egg production are classic clinical symptoms of Newcastle disease, a highly contagious and fatal viral disease in poultry. While other diseases can cause diarrhoea or reduced egg laying, the specific neurological manifestations are key indicators of Newcastle disease.
