  Controlling stocking density prevents overcrowding, which reduces the overall oxygen demand and accumulation of waste products in the pond. Aeration directly adds dissolved oxygen to the water, preventing depletion, especially during hot weather or at night when aquatic plants respire. These two practices are vital for maintaining healthy oxygen levels and overall water quality.
