  Proper site selection is fundamental for a successful poultry farm. Good drainage prevents waterlogging and disease spread, while isolation from residential areas minimizes nuisance (odour, noise) and reduces the risk of disease transmission between farms or from human activity. While other factors like market proximity and labour are important, biosecurity and environmental considerations are paramount for long-term sustainability and health of the flock.
