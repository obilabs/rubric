  Drip irrigation delivers water directly to the root zone of plants through a network of tubes and emitters, minimizing evaporation and runoff. This method is highly efficient in water use, making it ideal for regions with scarce water resources like Sokoto, unlike flood, furrow, or even sprinkler irrigation which have higher water loss rates.
