  Porcine Reproductive and Respiratory Syndrome (PRRS) is characterized by a combination of reproductive failures (abortions, mummified fetuses, small litter sizes, weak piglets) and respiratory problems in pigs of all ages. This specific set of symptoms, particularly the reproductive issues alongside respiratory signs, is highly indicative of a PRRS outbreak in a swine herd.

# Question
Mr. Emeka, a pig farmer in Enugu, notices that his sows frequently have small litter sizes and often abort their fetuses. The piglets born are weak and some are mummified. He also observes respiratory issues and infertility in some of his gilts. These symptoms collectively suggest an outbreak of which common swine disease?
## Choices
A. African Swine Fever (ASF)
B. Porcine Reproductive and Respiratory Syndrome (PRRS) *[CORRECT]*
C. Classical Swine Fever (CSF)
D. Swine Erysipelas
