explanation: |
  Terracing involves constructing level platforms or steps along the contours of a slope. This significantly reduces the speed of water runoff, prevents gully formation, and allows for better water infiltration, making it highly effective on steep terrains like the Mambilla Plateau. Mulching, contour ploughing, and strip cropping are useful but less effective than terracing for severe gully erosion on very steep slopes.
