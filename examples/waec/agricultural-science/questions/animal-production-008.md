explanation: |
  Endoparasites are parasites that live inside the body of their host. Tapeworms and roundworms are common examples of endoparasites found in the digestive tracts of livestock, causing nutrient malabsorption and other health issues. Ticks, lice, and mites are ectoparasites, living on the external surface of the host.
