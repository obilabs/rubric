  Newcastle disease is a highly contagious and often fatal viral disease affecting many species of birds, including domestic poultry. It is characterized by respiratory, nervous, and digestive signs. Coccidiosis and Fowl pox are also diseases, but Coccidiosis is parasitic and Fowl pox is viral but less systemically severe than Newcastle.
