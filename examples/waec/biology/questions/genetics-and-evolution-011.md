# Question
Mr. Emeka, a farmer in Benue State, crosses a pure-breeding tall cowpea plant with a pure-breeding dwarf cowpea plant. All the plants in the F1 generation are tall. If two plants from the F1 generation are crossed, what percentage of the F2 generation is expected to be dwarf?
## Choices
A. 0%
B. 25% *[CORRECT]*
C. 50%
D. 75%
