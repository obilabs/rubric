explanation: |
  Excessive nutrients from fertilizers leaching into water bodies cause eutrophication. This leads to an overgrowth of algae (algal bloom), which subsequently die and decompose. The decomposition process by bacteria consumes large amounts of dissolved oxygen, leading to oxygen depletion and the death of other aquatic organisms like fish.
