explanation: |
  While many cells (eukaryotic cells) do contain a nucleus and other membrane-bound organelles, this is not true for all cells. Prokaryotic cells, such as bacteria, lack a true nucleus and membrane-bound organelles. Therefore, stating that *all* cells have these structures is not a core tenet of the modern Cell Theory.
