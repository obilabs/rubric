explanation: |
  Abiotic factors are the non-living chemical and physical parts of the environment, such as temperature, sunlight, and dissolved oxygen. Biotic factors are the living or once-living components, such as fish, algae, and water hyacinth. Therefore, temperature is abiotic, and water hyacinth is biotic.
