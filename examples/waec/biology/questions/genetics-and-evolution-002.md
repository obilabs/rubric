  In a monohybrid cross where one parent is pure-breeding dominant (TT) and the other is pure-breeding recessive (tt), all offspring in the F1 generation will be heterozygous (Tt). Since 'T' (tall) is dominant over 't' (short), all F1 individuals will express the tall phenotype.
