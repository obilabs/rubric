explanation: |
  In plants, gaseous waste products like oxygen (during respiration) and carbon dioxide (during photosynthesis) are primarily excreted through diffusion. This occurs mainly through tiny pores on the leaf surface called stomata, and through lenticels, which are pores on stems and roots. Transpiration is the loss of water vapor.
