  In a reflex arc, the stimulus is detected by a receptor. The impulse then travels along a sensory neuron to the spinal cord (or brainstem), where it synapses with a relay neuron (interneuron). The relay neuron then transmits the impulse to a motor neuron, which carries it to an effector (muscle or gland) to produce a response.
