explanation: |
  Individuals with the sickle cell trait (heterozygous HbAS genotype) have a natural resistance to malaria because the presence of the HbS allele makes their red blood cells less hospitable to the malaria parasite. In regions where malaria is endemic, this resistance provides a significant survival advantage, leading to a higher frequency of the HbS allele in the population through natural selection, despite the severe effects of homozygous sickle cell disease. This is an example of balanced polymorphism.
