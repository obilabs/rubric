  A person with blood group AB has alleles I^A and I^B. A person with blood group O has two recessive 'i' alleles. When they cross, the possible genotypes for their children are I^Ai (Blood Group A) and I^Bi (Blood Group B). Therefore, their children can only have blood groups A or B.
