  Gibberellins are a class of plant hormones that play a crucial role in regulating various growth processes. Their primary functions include promoting stem elongation by stimulating cell division and elongation, breaking seed dormancy, and promoting seed germination. Auxins are more involved in root growth and apical dominance.
