explanation: |
  Auxins, produced primarily in the apical bud, are responsible for apical dominance, where they inhibit the growth of lateral buds. When the apical bud is pruned, the source of auxin is removed, thus releasing the inhibition on lateral buds and allowing them to grow more vigorously.
