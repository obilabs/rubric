  First, convert the final velocity from km/h to m/s: 72 km/h = 72 * (1000 m / 3600 s) = 20 m/s.
  Since the car accelerates from rest, the initial velocity (u) is 0 m/s. The acceleration (a) is calculated using the formula a = (v - u) / t, where v is final velocity, u is initial velocity, and t is time.
  a = (20 m/s - 0 m/s) / 10 s = 2.0 m/s².

# Question

## Choices

B. 7.2 m/s²
