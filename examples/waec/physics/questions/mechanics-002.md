  Pressure is defined as force per unit area. It is calculated by dividing the total force applied by the area over which the force is distributed. The formula is P = F/A.

# Question
A student in Kaduna, weighing 600 N, stands on one leg. If the area of contact of her shoe with the ground is 0.005 m², what pressure does she exert on the ground?
## Choices
A. 3000 N/m²
B. 120,000 N/m² *[CORRECT]*
C. 600.005 N/m²
D. 0.0000083 N/m²
