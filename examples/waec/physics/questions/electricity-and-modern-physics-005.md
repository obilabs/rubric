explanation: |
  For resistors in parallel, the reciprocal of the equivalent resistance (1/Rp) is the sum of the reciprocals of the individual resistances.
  1/Rp = 1/R1 + 1/R2 + 1/R3 = 1/6Ω + 1/3Ω + 1/2Ω.
  To add these fractions, find a common denominator, which is 6.
  1/Rp = 1/6 + 2/6 + 3/6 = 6/6 = 1.
  Therefore, Rp = 1/1 = 1Ω.
