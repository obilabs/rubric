  The heat required to melt a substance at its melting point is given by the formula Q = mL_f, where Q is the heat energy, m is the mass, and L_f is the specific latent heat of fusion. First, convert the mass from grams to kilograms (500 g = 0.5 kg). Then, Q = 0.5 kg × 3.36 × 10^5 J/kg = 1.68 × 10^5 J.

# Question
Ngozi, a student in Kano, wants to melt 500 g of ice at 0°C into water at 0°C to mix with her juice. If the specific latent heat of fusion of ice is 3.36 × 10^5 J/kg, how much heat energy is absorbed by the ice?
## Choices
A. 1.68 × 10^2 J
B. 1.68 × 10^3 J
C. 1.68 × 10^5 J *[CORRECT]*
D. 1.68 × 10^8 J
