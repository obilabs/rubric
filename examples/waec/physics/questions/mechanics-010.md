  Power is the rate at which work is done, or energy is transferred. It is calculated as Work/time or Force × velocity (P = Fv). Given force = 100 N and velocity = 2 m/s, Power = 100 N × 2 m/s = 200 W.

# Question
A worker at the Dangote Cement factory in Obajana pushes a trolley with a constant force of 100 N at a constant velocity of 2 m/s. What is the power exerted by the worker?
## Choices
A. 50 W
B. 200 W *[CORRECT]*
C. 100 W
D. 400 W
