  The area under a velocity-time graph represents the displacement of the object. For the given graph, the area is a trapezium. Area = ½ × (sum of parallel sides) × height = ½ × (30+10) × 20 = ½ × 40 × 20 = 400 m.

# Question
A Keke Napep starts from rest and accelerates uniformly to 20 m/s in 10 seconds. It then maintains this velocity for another 20 seconds before decelerating uniformly to rest in 5 seconds. What is the total distance covered during the initial 10 seconds of acceleration?
## Choices
A. 100 m *[CORRECT]*
B. 200 m
C. 300 m
D. 400 m
