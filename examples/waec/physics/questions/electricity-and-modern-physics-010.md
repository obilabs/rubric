explanation: |
  Alpha particles consist of two protons and two neutrons, meaning they have a charge of +2e. They are relatively massive and have low penetrating power but high ionizing power. Beta particles are high-energy electrons or positrons, with charge -e or +e, moderate penetrating power, and moderate ionizing power. Gamma rays are high-energy electromagnetic radiation with no charge or mass, very high penetrating power, and low ionizing power.
