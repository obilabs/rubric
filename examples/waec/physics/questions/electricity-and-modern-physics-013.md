  The number of half-lives is the total time divided by the half-life, which is 72 days / 24 days = 3 half-lives. For each half-life, the amount of radioactive isotope reduces by half. Starting with 80 g, after 3 half-lives, the remaining amount will be 80 g * (1/2)^3 = 80 g * (1/8) = 10 g.

# Question
The half-life of a radioactive isotope, Thorium-234, is 24 days. If a sample initially contains 80 g of Thorium-234, how much of the isotope will remain after 72 days?
## Choices
A. 40 g
B. 20 g
C. 10 g *[CORRECT]*
D. 5 g
