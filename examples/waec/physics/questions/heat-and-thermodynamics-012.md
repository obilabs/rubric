explanation: |
  According to the Pressure Law (Gay-Lussac's Law), for a fixed mass of gas at constant volume, pressure is directly proportional to its absolute temperature (P₁/T₁ = P₂/T₂). First, convert temperatures from Celsius to Kelvin: T₁ = 27°C + 273 = 300 K, and T₂ = 77°C + 273 = 350 K. Then, P₂ = P₁ × (T₂/T₁) = (2.0 × 10^5 Pa) × (350 K / 300 K) = 2.0 × 10^5 Pa × (7/6) ≈ 2.33 × 10^5 Pa.
