  First, find the total resistance of the parallel combination: 1/Rp = 1/6 + 1/12 = 2/12 + 1/12 = 3/12 = 1/4. So, Rp = 4Ω.
  The total resistance of the circuit is R_total = 4Ω (parallel) + 8Ω (series) = 12Ω.
  Using Ohm's Law, the total current (I) from the battery is V/R_total = 24V / 12Ω = 2A.
  This 2A current flows through the 8Ω resistor.
  The potential difference across the parallel combination is Vp = I × Rp = 2A × 4Ω = 8V.
  The current through the 6Ω resistor is I6 = Vp / 6Ω = 8V / 6Ω = 4/3 A ≈ 1.33 A.
