  During a phase change (like melting or boiling), the heat energy supplied is used to break the intermolecular bonds and change the state of the substance, not to increase its kinetic energy. Therefore, the temperature remains constant.
---
# Question
When a substance is undergoing a phase change, such as melting or boiling, what happens to its temperature if heat is continuously supplied?
## Choices
A. The temperature increases steadily.
B. The temperature remains constant. *[CORRECT]*
C. The temperature decreases temporarily.
D. The temperature fluctuates randomly.
