# Question
A ceiling fan in Mr. Segun's room operates at 75 W. If he uses the fan for 8 hours daily for 30 days, and the cost of electricity is N50.00 per kilowatt-hour (kWh), what is his electricity bill for using the fan for the month?
## Choices
A. N1,200.00
B. N900.00 *[CORRECT]*
C. N750.00
