explanation: |
  The work done (W) by the student against gravity is equal to the change in potential energy, W = mgh, where m is mass, g is acceleration due to gravity, and h is height.
  W = 50 kg * 10 m/s² * 6 m = 3000 Joules.
  Power (P) is the rate at which work is done, P = W / t, where t is time.
  P = 3000 J / 12 s = 250 Watts.
---
# Question
A student of mass 50 kg climbs a flight of stairs 6 m high in 12 seconds in a multi-storey building in Abuja. Calculate the power developed by the student. (Take g = 10 m/s²).
## Choices
A. 25 W
B. 50 W
C. 250 W *[CORRECT]*
D. 500 W
