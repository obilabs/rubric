  In projectile motion, the horizontal component of velocity remains constant throughout the flight, neglecting air resistance, because there is no horizontal force acting on the projectile. The vertical component changes due to gravity.

# Question
A stone is thrown horizontally from the top of Olumo Rock. Neglecting air resistance, which of the following statements about its motion is correct?
## Choices
A. Its vertical velocity remains constant.
B. Its horizontal velocity continuously increases.
C. Its horizontal velocity remains constant. *[CORRECT]*
D. Its acceleration is zero at the highest point.
