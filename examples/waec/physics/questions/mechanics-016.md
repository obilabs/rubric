explanation: |
  According to Hooke's Law, the force (F) applied to a spring is directly proportional to its extension (e), i.e., F = ke, where k is the spring constant.
  First, calculate the extension (e): e = final length - original length = 24 cm - 20 cm = 4 cm.
  Convert the extension to meters: 4 cm = 0.04 m.
  Now, rearrange the formula to find the spring constant: k = F / e.
  k = 80 N / 0.04 m = 2000 N/m.

# Question
A spring in a weighing scale at a local market has an original length of 20 cm. When a load of 80 N is attached to it, its length extends to 24 cm. What is the spring constant?
## Choices
A. 20 N/m
B. 2000 N/m *[CORRECT]*
C. 320 N/m
D. 3200 N/m
