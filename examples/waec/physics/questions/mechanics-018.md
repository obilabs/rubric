explanation: |
  Mass is an intrinsic property of an object and remains constant regardless of the gravitational field. Weight, however, is the force of gravity acting on an object (Weight = mass × g).
  First, find the astronaut's mass on Earth: Mass = Weight on Earth / g_Earth = 700 N / 10 N/kg = 70 kg.
  Since mass is constant, her mass on the Moon is also 70 kg.
  Now, calculate her weight on the Moon: Weight on Moon = Mass × g_Moon = 70 kg × 1.6 N/kg = 112 N.

# Question
An astronaut from Nigeria, Dr. Arinze, weighs 700 N on Earth where g = 10 N/kg. If the acceleration due to gravity on the Moon is 1.6 N/kg, what would be her mass and weight on the Moon, respectively?
