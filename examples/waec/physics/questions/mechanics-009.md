explanation: |
  For the block to be in equilibrium, the net force acting on it must be zero. The frictional force (f) opposes the tendency of motion. The maximum static frictional force is given by f_max = μ_s * N, where N is the normal force. Here, N = mg = 5 kg * 10 m/s² = 50 N. So, f_max = 0.4 * 50 N = 20 N. Since the applied force (15 N) is less than the maximum static frictional force (20 N), the block will remain at rest, and the frictional force will be equal to the applied force.

# Question
A block of mass 5 kg rests on a rough horizontal table. The coefficient of static friction between the block and the table is 0.4. If a horizontal force of 15 N is applied to the block, what is the magnitude of the frictional force acting on the block? (Take g = 10 m/s²)
## Choices
A. 15 N *[CORRECT]*
B. 20 N
C. 50 N
D. 0 N
