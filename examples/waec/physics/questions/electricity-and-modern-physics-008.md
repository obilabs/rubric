  An electromagnet produces a magnetic field only when current flows through its coil. This allows its magnetic field to be turned on or off, making it suitable for applications like lifting heavy scrap metal where the magnetism needs to be controlled. A permanent magnet cannot be switched off.

# Question

## Choices
A. is much stronger than any permanent magnet.
B. never loses its magnetism.
C. can be switched on and off. *[CORRECT]*
D. consumes less electricity.
