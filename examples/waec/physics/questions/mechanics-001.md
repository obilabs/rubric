  Acceleration is the rate of change of velocity. If a vehicle maintains a constant velocity, its speed and direction are not changing. Therefore, the change in velocity is zero, resulting in zero acceleration.

# Question
A new bus, "Peace Mass Transit," travels from Enugu to Abuja maintaining a constant velocity of 100 km/h for a significant portion of the journey. What is its acceleration during this period?
## Choices
A. Zero *[CORRECT]*
B. 100 km/h²
C. Increasing
D. Decreasing
