  After 2 hours (120 minutes), the mass remaining is 1/8 of the original mass. This means the sample has undergone 3 half-lives, because (1/2)^3 = 1/8.
  Therefore, 3 half-lives = 120 minutes.
  One half-life = 120 minutes / 3 = 40 minutes.

# Question
A radioactive isotope used in medical imaging in Lagos has a mass of 80 g. After 2 hours, its mass reduces to 10 g. What is the half-life of the isotope?
## Choices
A. 20 minutes
B. 30 minutes
C. 40 minutes *[CORRECT]*
