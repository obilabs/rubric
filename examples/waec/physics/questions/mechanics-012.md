explanation: |
  Newton's Second Law of Motion states that force (F) is equal to mass (m) times acceleration (a), or F = ma.
  Given F = 50 N and m = 10 kg, we can rearrange the formula to find acceleration: a = F / m.
  a = 50 N / 10 kg = 5.0 m/s².

# Question

B. 5.0 m/s² *[CORRECT]*
