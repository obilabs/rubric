explanation: |
  For resistors connected in parallel, the reciprocal of the equivalent resistance (1/Req) is the sum of the reciprocals of individual resistances (1/R1 + 1/R2 + 1/R3). Calculating this, 1/Req = 1/4 + 1/6 + 1/12 = 3/12 + 2/12 + 1/12 = 6/12 = 1/2. Therefore, Req = 2 Ω.
