  For an ideal transformer, the ratio of voltages is equal to the ratio of the number of turns in the coils (Vs/Vp = Ns/Np). Rearranging for Vs, we get Vs = Vp * (Ns/Np). Substituting the values, Vs = 11000 V * (100/2000) = 550 V.

# Question

B. 550 V *[CORRECT]*
