explanation: |
  The pressure at a certain depth in a liquid is given by the formula P = ρgh, where ρ is the density of the liquid, g is the acceleration due to gravity, and h is the depth.
  From this formula, pressure depends on the density of the liquid, the depth below the surface, and the acceleration due to gravity. It does not depend on the surface area of the liquid.
