explanation: |
  The photoelectric effect describes the emission of electrons from a material when light shines on it. Solar panels (photovoltaic cells) work on this principle, converting light energy directly into electrical energy. When photons from sunlight strike the semiconductor material in the panel, they provide enough energy to release electrons, creating an electric current.
