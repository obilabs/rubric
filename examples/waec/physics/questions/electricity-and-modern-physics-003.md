explanation: |
  For a step-down transformer, the ratio of primary voltage to secondary voltage is equal to the ratio of primary turns to secondary turns (Vp/Vs = Np/Ns).
  Given Vp = 240 V, Np = 1200 turns, Ns = 300 turns.
  Vs = Vp × (Ns/Np) = 240 V × (300/1200) = 240 V × (1/4) = 60 V.
---
# Question
A step-down transformer used in a small electronics workshop in Abuja has 1200 turns in its primary coil and 300 turns in its secondary coil. If the primary coil is connected to a 240 V AC mains supply, what is the output voltage across the secondary coil?
## Choices
A. 60 V *[CORRECT]*
B. 120 V
C. 480 V
D. 960 V
