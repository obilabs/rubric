  Nuclear fission is the splitting of a heavy nucleus into lighter nuclei, releasing a large amount of energy. Nuclear fusion is the joining of two light nuclei to form a heavier nucleus, also releasing a large amount of energy, and is the process that powers the sun. Both processes release enormous amounts of energy.
