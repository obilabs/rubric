  First, find the area of the rectangular room: Area = length × width = 8 m × 5 m = 40 m².
  Next, find the area of one square tile: Area = side × side = 0.5 m × 0.5 m = 0.25 m².
  Number of tiles needed = Total room area / Area of one tile = 40 m² / 0.25 m² = 160 tiles.
  Total cost = Number of tiles × Cost per tile = 160 × ₦500 = ₦80,000.

# Question
