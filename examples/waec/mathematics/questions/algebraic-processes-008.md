  To find the gradient, rearrange the equation into the form $y = mx + c$, where 'm' is the gradient.
  Given $2y = -6x + 8$, divide all terms by 2: $y = -3x + 4$. The coefficient of $x$ is -3, which is the gradient.

# Question
What is the gradient of the straight line represented by the equation $2y = -6x + 8$?
## Choices
A. 8
B. 4
C. -6
D. -3 *[CORRECT]*
