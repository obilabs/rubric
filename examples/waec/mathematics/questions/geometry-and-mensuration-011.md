explanation: |
  The sum of angles in a triangle is always 180°. Given angle A = 50° and angle B = 70°, we can find angle C.
  Therefore, angle C = 180° - (50° + 70°) = 180° - 120° = 60°.

# Question
In triangle PQR, angle P = 50° and angle Q = 70°. What is the measure of angle R?
## Choices
A. 60° *[CORRECT]*
B. 70°
C. 80°
D. 90°
