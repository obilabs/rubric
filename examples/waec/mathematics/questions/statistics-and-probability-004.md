# Question
A bag contains 5 red beads, 3 blue beads, and 7 green beads. If a bead is picked at random from the bag, what is the probability that it is a red bead?
## Choices
A. 1/5
B. 1/15
C. 5/7
D. 1/3 *[CORRECT]*
