explanation: |
  Since Y varies directly as A, we can write $Y = kA$. Using the given values, $300 = k \times 5$, so the constant of proportionality $k = 300/5 = 60$.
  Now, for 8 hectares, $Y = 60 \times 8 = 480$ yams.

# Question
The number of yams, Y, produced by a farm varies directly as the area cultivated, A. If a farm produces 300 yams from an area of 5 hectares, how many yams will it produce from 8 hectares?
## Choices
A. 480 yams *[CORRECT]*
B. 500 yams
C. 600 yams
D. 750 yams
