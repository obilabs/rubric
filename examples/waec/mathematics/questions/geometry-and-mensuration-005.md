explanation: |
  The volume of a cylinder is given by the formula V = πr²h.
  Given radius (r) = 7 cm and height (h) = 10 cm. Using π = 22/7.
  V = (22/7) × (7 cm)² × 10 cm = (22/7) × 49 cm² × 10 cm.
  V = 22 × 7 cm² × 10 cm = 154 cm² × 10 cm = 1540 cm³.

# Question
A cylindrical tank used for storing water in a Nigerian home has a radius of 7 cm and a height of 10 cm. Calculate its volume, taking π = 22/7.
## Choices
A. 220 cm³
B. 440 cm³
C. 770 cm³
D. 1540 cm³*[CORRECT]*
