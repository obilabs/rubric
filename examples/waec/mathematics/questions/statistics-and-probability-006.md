explanation: |
  This is a permutation problem because the order of arrangement matters. There are 6 distinct books to be arranged on a shelf. The number of ways to arrange 'n' distinct items is n!. So, for 6 books, it is 6! = 6 × 5 × 4 × 3 × 2 × 1 = 720 ways.
---
# Question
In how many distinct ways can Emeka arrange 6 different textbooks on his study shelf?
## Choices
A. 36
B. 720 *[CORRECT]*
C. 120
D. 6
