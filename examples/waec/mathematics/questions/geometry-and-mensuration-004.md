explanation: |
  The angle subtended by a diameter at any point on the circumference of a circle is always a right angle (90°).
  Since AB is the diameter, the angle at the circumference, ∠ACB, must be 90°.
  In triangle ABC, the sum of angles is 180°. So, ∠BAC + ∠ABC + ∠ACB = 180°.
  Given ∠BAC = 55° and ∠ACB = 90°, then 55° + ∠ABC + 90° = 180°.
  145° + ∠ABC = 180°, which means ∠ABC = 180° - 145° = 35°.

# Question
In a circle with center O, AB is a diameter and C is a point on the circumference. If ∠BAC = 55°, what is ∠ABC?
## Choices
A. 45°
B. 35°*[CORRECT]*
C. 55°
D. 70°
