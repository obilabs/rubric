  To find the bearing of B from A, we add or subtract 180° from the bearing of A from B.
  If the bearing of A from B is less than 180°, add 180°. If it's greater than 180°, subtract 180°.
  Given bearing of A from B = 215°. Since 215° > 180°, we subtract 180°.
  Bearing of B from A = 215° - 180° = 035°.
