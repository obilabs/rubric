  Let the first term be 'a' and the common difference be 'd'. From T5 = a + 4d = 17 and T10 = a + 9d = 32, subtracting the first from the second gives 5d = 15, so d = 3. Substituting d=3 into a+4d=17 yields a=5. The sum of the first 15 terms is S15 = 15/2 * (2a + (15-1)d) = 15/2 * (2*5 + 14*3) = 15/2 * (10 + 42) = 15/2 * 52 = 15 * 26 = 390.
