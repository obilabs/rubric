  First, calculate the mean: (10+12+14+16+18) / 5 = 70 / 5 = 14.
  Next, find the squared differences from the mean:
  (10-14)² = (-4)² = 16
  (12-14)² = (-2)² = 4
  (14-14)² = (0)² = 0
  (16-14)² = (2)² = 4
  (18-14)² = (4)² = 16
  Sum of squared differences = 16 + 4 + 0 + 4 + 16 = 40.
  Variance = (Sum of squared differences) / (number of data points) = 40 / 5 = 8.
