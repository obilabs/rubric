  Let Aisha's money be A and Bayo's money be B. We have two equations: A + B = ₦2500 and A = B + ₦500.
  Substitute the second equation into the first: $(B + ₦500) + B = ₦2500$. This simplifies to $2B + ₦500 = ₦2500$, so $2B = ₦2000$.
  Therefore, $B = ₦1000$.
