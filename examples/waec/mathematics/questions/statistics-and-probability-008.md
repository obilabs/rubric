explanation: |
  Let P(Maths) be the probability of passing Mathematics and P(English) be the probability of passing English.
  P(Maths) = 0.7, P(English) = 0.8.
  Since the events are independent, the probability of passing both is P(Maths AND English) = P(Maths) × P(English) = 0.7 × 0.8 = 0.56.
  The probability of passing AT LEAST one subject is P(Maths OR English) = P(Maths) + P(English) - P(Maths AND English).
  P(Maths OR English) = 0.7 + 0.8 - 0.56 = 1.5 - 0.56 = 0.94.

# Question
The probability that a student, Tunde, passes Mathematics is 0.7, and the probability that he passes English Language is 0.8. If the two events are independent, what is the probability that Tunde passes at least one of the subjects?
## Choices
A. 0.56
B. 0.14
C. 0.90
D. 0.94 *[CORRECT]*
