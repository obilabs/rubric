explanation: |
  The angle subtended by an arc at the centre of a circle is twice the angle subtended by the same arc at any point on the remaining part of the circumference.
  Therefore, angle ABC (at the circumference) = (Angle AOC (at the centre)) / 2 = 120° / 2 = 60°.
---
# Question
In the circle with centre O, points A, B, C are on the circumference. If angle AOC = 120°, find angle ABC.
## Choices
A. 30°
B. 45°
C. 60° *[CORRECT]*
D. 90°
