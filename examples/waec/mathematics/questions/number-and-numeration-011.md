  First, find the total fraction of fabric used by adding the individual fractions: 3/8 + 1/4 = 3/8 + 2/8 = 5/8. Since the roll was initially full (representing 1 whole), subtract the used portion from 1 to find the remaining fraction: 1 - 5/8 = 3/8.

# Question
A tailor used 3/8 of a roll of ankara fabric for a customer and 1/4 of the roll for another. What fraction of the ankara roll is left if the roll was initially full?
## Choices
A. 3/8 *[CORRECT]*
B. 5/8
C. 1/2
D. 7/8
