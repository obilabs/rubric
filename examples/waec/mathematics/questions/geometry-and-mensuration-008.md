  The distance between two points along the same parallel of latitude is given by the formula: Distance = (θ/360) × 2πR cos(latitude), where θ is the difference in longitude and R is the radius of the Earth (6400 km).
  The difference in longitude (θ) = 15°W - 5°E = 15° + 5° = 20°. (When one is West and the other East, sum them).
  Distance = (20/360) × 2 × (22/7) × 6400 × cos(60°).
  Distance = (1/18) × 2 × (22/7) × 6400 × 0.5.
  Distance = (1/18) × (22/7) × 6400 = 2252.38... ≈ 2253 km.
