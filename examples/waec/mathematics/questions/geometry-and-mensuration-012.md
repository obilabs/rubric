  The area of a trapezium is given by the formula 0.5 * (sum of parallel sides) * height. So, Area = 0.5 * (25m + 35m) * 15m = 450 m².
  The total cost of the plot is then the area multiplied by the cost per square meter. Total cost = 450 m² * ₦10,000/m² = ₦4,500,000.

# Question
A plot of land in Abuja is in the shape of a trapezium. The parallel sides measure 25m and 35m, and the perpendicular distance between them is 15m. If the cost of land is ₦10,000 per square meter, what is the total cost of the plot?
## Choices
A. ₦3,750,000
B. ₦4,500,000 *[CORRECT]*
C. ₦9,000,000
D. ₦5,250,000
