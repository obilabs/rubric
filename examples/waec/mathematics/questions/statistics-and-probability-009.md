  This is a combination problem because the order of selecting the delegates does not matter. We need to choose 3 delegates from 10 people. The formula for combinations is nCr = n! / (r! * (n-r)!).
  Here, n = 10 and r = 3.
  10C3 = 10! / (3! * (10-3)!) = 10! / (3! * 7!) = (10 × 9 × 8 × 7!) / ((3 × 2 × 1) × 7!) = (10 × 9 × 8) / (3 × 2 × 1) = 10 × 3 × 4 = 120 ways.
