# Question
At a market in Ibadan, Bola bought 2 pineapples and 3 mangoes for ₦1,200. Femi bought 3 pineapples and 1 mango for ₦1,100. If 'p' represents the cost of a pineapple and 'm' represents the cost of a mango, find the cost of one mango.
## Choices
A. ₦300.00
B. ₦250.00
C. ₦200.00 *[CORRECT]*
