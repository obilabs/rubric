  First, calculate the area used for yam: (2/5) * 20 hectares = 8 hectares. Next, calculate the area used for cassava: (1/4) * 20 hectares = 5 hectares. The total area for yam and cassava is 8 + 5 = 13 hectares. The rest of the farm is for maize, so subtract the combined area from the total farm size: 20 - 13 = 7 hectares.

# Question
A farmer in Benue State cultivates yam, cassava, and maize on his 20-hectare farm. He uses 2/5 of the farm for yam, 1/4 for cassava, and the rest for maize. How many hectares are used for maize?
## Choices
A. 6 hectares
B. 7 hectares *[CORRECT]*
C. 8 hectares
