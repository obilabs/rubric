  Let 'x' be the number of bags of rice. The total earnings from selling 'x' bags is $30000x$.
  "At least ₦180,000" means the earnings must be greater than or equal to ₦180,000. So, $30000x \geq 180000$.

# Question
A trader sells bags of rice for ₦30,000 each. If she wants to make at least ₦180,000 from selling 'x' bags of rice, which inequality represents the situation?
## Choices
A. $30000x < 180000$
B. $30000x = 180000$
C. $30000x \geq 180000$ *[CORRECT]*
D. $30000x \leq 180000$
