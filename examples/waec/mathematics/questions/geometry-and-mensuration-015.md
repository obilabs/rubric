explanation: |
  The movement north corresponds to the adjacent side of a right-angled triangle, where the hypotenuse is the distance sailed and the angle is relative to the north line.
  Using trigonometry, distance north = hypotenuse * cos(angle). So, 100 km * cos(60°) = 100 km * 0.5 = 50 km.
---
# Question
A ship sails from Port Harcourt on a bearing of 060° for a distance of 100 km. How far north of Port Harcourt is the ship?
## Choices
A. 50 km *[CORRECT]*
B. 86.6 km
C. 100 km
D. 115.5 km
