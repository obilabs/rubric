explanation: |
  The sum of the exterior angles of any convex polygon is always 360°.
  For a regular polygon with 'n' sides, all exterior angles are equal.
  Therefore, each exterior angle = 360° / n.
  Given that each exterior angle is 45°, we have 45° = 360° / n.
  Solving for n, n = 360° / 45° = 8.
---
