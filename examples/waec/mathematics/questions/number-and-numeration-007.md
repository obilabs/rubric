  The formula for the nth term of an Arithmetic Progression is T_n = a + (n-1)d, where 'a' is the first term, 'n' is the term number, and 'd' is the common difference. Given a = 5, d = 3, and n = 12. Substitute these values: T_12 = 5 + (12-1) * 3 = 5 + 11 * 3 = 5 + 33 = 38.

# Question
The first term of an Arithmetic Progression (A.P.) is 5 and the common difference is 3. Find the 12th term.
## Choices
A. 38 *[CORRECT]*
B. 35
C. 41
D. 32
