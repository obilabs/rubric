  Adewale's age = x. Father's age = 3x. Mother's age = 3x - 5.
  The sum of their ages is $x + 3x + (3x - 5)$.
  Combining like terms, we get $(x + 3x + 3x) - 5 = 7x - 5$.

# Question
Adewale is x years old. His father is 3 times Adewale's age, and his mother is 5 years younger than his father. What is the sum of their ages?
## Choices
A. $(7x - 5)$ years *[CORRECT]*
B. $(4x - 5)$ years
C. $(3x - 5)$ years
D. $(7x + 5)$ years
