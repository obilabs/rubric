explanation: |
  For a Geometric Progression, the nth term is given by T_n = ar^(n-1). We have T_3 = ar^(3-1) = ar^2 = 12 and T_6 = ar^(6-1) = ar^5 = 96. Divide the equation for T_6 by the equation for T_3: (ar^5) / (ar^2) = 96 / 12. This simplifies to r^3 = 8. Taking the cube root of both sides, r = 2.

# Question
The 3rd term of a Geometric Progression (G.P.) is 12 and the 6th term is 96. Find the common ratio.
## Choices
A. 1/2
B. 3
C. 4
D. 2 *[CORRECT]*
