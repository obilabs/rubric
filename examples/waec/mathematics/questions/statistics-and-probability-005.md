  First, find the total number of students: 10 + 15 + 8 + 7 = 40 students. The number of students who prefer Garri is 15. The angle for a sector in a pie chart is (frequency / total frequency) * 360°. So, for Garri, the angle is (15 / 40) * 360° = (3/8) * 360° = 3 * 45° = 135°.
---
# Question
A survey was conducted among 40 students at a school canteen to determine their favourite staple food. The results are shown below:
*   Pounded Yam: 10 students
*   Garri: 15 students
*   Amala: 8 students
*   Semo: 7 students

If this data is represented in a pie chart, what is the angle of the sector for Garri?
## Choices
A. 135° *[CORRECT]*
B. 90°
C. 120°
D. 72°
