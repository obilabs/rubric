explanation: |
  "Specious" means superficially plausible, but actually wrong or misleading. In the context of promises, it describes those that appear good or convincing but are deceptive or not genuine. "Genuine," "sincere," and "profound" are positive or unrelated attributes.
---
# Question
The politician's campaign was full of ______ promises that he had no intention of keeping.
## Choices
A. genuine
B. sincere
C. profound
D. specious *[CORRECT]*
