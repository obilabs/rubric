  In sentences connected by "neither... nor...", the verb agrees with the subject closest to it. In this case, "teachers" is plural, so the plural verb "are" is correct.
---
# Question
Choose the option that *best completes* the gap.

Neither the principal nor the teachers ______ aware of the new policy on school uniforms.
## Choices
A. was
B. is
C. am
D. are *[CORRECT]*
