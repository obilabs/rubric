  A minimal pair consists of two words that differ by only one sound and have different meanings. *Pat* (/pæt/) and *bat* (/bæt/) differ only in their initial consonant sounds, /p/ and /b/ respectively, thus forming a minimal pair. The other pairs differ by more than one sound or different types of sounds.
