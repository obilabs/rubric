explanation: |
  The most appropriate preposition to describe being within the general area or confines of a market is 'in'. While one might walk 'along' or 'across' parts of a market, the vendor is selling her wares 'in' the market as a whole. 'On' is incorrect as it implies being on top of a surface.
---
# Question
Despite the heavy downpour, the vendor continued to hawk her wares __________ the market.
## Choices
A. along
B. in *[CORRECT]*
C. on
D. across
