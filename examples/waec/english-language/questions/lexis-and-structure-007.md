explanation: |
  'Inflammatory' remarks are those intended to arouse anger, hostility, or passion. In a tense situation, such remarks would naturally cause more tension. 'Conciliatory' means intended to placate, and 'benign' and 'innocuous' mean harmless, which are antonyms in this context.
---
# Question
Choose the word or phrase that *best completes* the sentence.

The politician's ______ remarks about the recent election results caused a lot of tension in the already polarized room.
## Choices
A. conciliatory
B. benign
C. inflammatory *[CORRECT]*
D. innocuous
