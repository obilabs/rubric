  'Benevolent' means well meaning and kindly. The opposite of being kind and well-meaning is to be cruel or malevolent. 'Cruel' perfectly captures this opposite meaning.

# Question
From the options lettered A to D, choose the word or phrase that is *opposite in meaning* to the underlined word as used in the sentence.

The community praised the new monarch for his **benevolent** rule, which brought peace and prosperity to the land.
## Choices
A. cruel *[CORRECT]*
B. generous
C. lenient
D. kind
