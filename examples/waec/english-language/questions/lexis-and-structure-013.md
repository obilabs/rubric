domains: [Lexis and Structure]
difficulty: HARD
explanation: |
  "Perilous" means full of danger or risk. Therefore, its antonym is "safe," which means protected from danger or risk. "Hazardous" is a synonym, while "difficult" and "exciting" are not direct antonyms.
---
# Question
The journey through the dense forest to the remote village was **perilous**.
## Choices
A. hazardous
B. safe *[CORRECT]*
C. difficult
D. exciting
