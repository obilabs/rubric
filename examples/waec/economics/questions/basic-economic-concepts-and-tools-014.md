  The mean (average) is calculated by summing all the values in a dataset and then dividing by the number of values. For the given incomes (N7,000 + N9,000 + N8,000 + N12,000 + N14,000 = N50,000), dividing by the number of vendors (5) gives N50,000 / 5 = N10,000.

# Question
The weekly incomes (in Naira) of five street vendors in Lagos are N7,000, N9,000, N8,000, N12,000, and N14,000. What is the mean weekly income of these vendors?
## Choices
A. N10,000 *[CORRECT]*
B. N9,000
C. N12,000
D. N10,500
