# Question
Bola has N2,000 and wants to buy either a new Economics textbook or attend a concert. If she chooses to buy the textbook, what is her opportunity cost?
## Choices
A. The N2,000 spent on the textbook.
B. The satisfaction she gets from reading the textbook.
C. Attending the concert. *[CORRECT]*
D. The price of the concert ticket.
