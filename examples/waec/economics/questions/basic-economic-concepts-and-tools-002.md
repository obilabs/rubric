  Opportunity cost is the value of the next best alternative that was not chosen when a decision was made. In this scenario, Aisha chose to buy rice, so the opportunity cost is the beans she could have bought with the same N5,000.

# Question
Aisha has N5,000 to spend. She can either buy a bag of local rice or a bag of beans. If she decides to buy the bag of rice, what is her opportunity cost?
## Choices
A. The N5,000 she spent on the rice.
B. The bag of beans she could have bought. *[CORRECT]*
C. The satisfaction she gets from eating rice.
D. The effort she put into deciding what to buy.
