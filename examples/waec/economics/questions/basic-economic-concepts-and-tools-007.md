  The mean is calculated by summing all the values and dividing by the number of values. For the given incomes (N40,000 + N55,000 + N60,000 + N85,000 + N110,000) = N350,000. Dividing by 5 individuals gives a mean of N70,000.

# Question
Five individuals in a community in Abia State have monthly incomes of N40,000, N55,000, N60,000, N85,000, and N110,000 respectively. What is the mean (average) income of these individuals?
## Choices
A. N60,000
B. N65,000
C. N70,000 *[CORRECT]*
D. N85,000
