# Question

Which of the following are the three components of the CIA triad? *(Select THREE)*

## Choices

A. Confidentiality *[CORRECT]*
> Keeping data secret from unauthorized parties — encryption and access control serve this.
B. Integrity *[CORRECT]*
> Assurance that data has not been altered — hashing and digital signatures serve this.
C. Availability *[CORRECT]*
> Ensuring systems and data are reachable when needed — redundancy and DDoS defence serve this.
D. Authentication
> A real security property, but not part of the triad. This is the classic distractor:
> auth *supports* confidentiality, it isn't one of the three legs.
E. Non-repudiation
> Also genuine, and also not in the triad. It belongs to the extended "AAA"/assurance
> family, not CIA.
