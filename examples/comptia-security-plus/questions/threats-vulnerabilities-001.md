# Question

An accounts-payable clerk receives an urgent email that appears to be from the CFO,
demanding an immediate wire transfer to a new vendor before end of day. Which attack
does this BEST describe?

## Choices

A. Business email compromise *[CORRECT]*
> Impersonation of a trusted executive plus manufactured urgency to bypass normal
> approval — the hallmark of BEC.
B. Denial of service
> DoS attacks availability by exhausting resources; nothing here targets uptime.
C. SQL injection
> An injection attack manipulates a database query — this email never touches an application input.
D. Privilege escalation
> That is gaining higher access on a system already breached; the attacker here is
> manipulating a person, not elevating an account.
