  and glucose are products, not inputs.

# Question

Which of the following are *inputs* (reactants) of photosynthesis? *(Select TWO)*

## Choices

A. Carbon dioxide *[CORRECT]*
> Taken in through the stomata; its carbon becomes the carbon in glucose.
B. Oxygen
> A common trap: oxygen is a *product* of photosynthesis, released as waste. It
> is an input of respiration — the reverse process — which is why the two are
> easy to confuse.
C. Water *[CORRECT]*
> Drawn up from the roots; split during the light reactions to supply electrons.
D. Glucose
> Glucose is the *output* — the stored chemical energy the plant makes. Marking
> it as an input reverses the whole reaction.
